Everything here is free to download, print and use with your crew. These are made to live on walls: print them at A3, at 100%. Black and white is fine.

There are three posters (the story, the tools and the muscles) and seven worksheets for working on together. The tools are best used with a few people, so there's some healthy disagreement.

The Inner Landscape: tools for better conversations

These three are card decks, so there's nothing to print. They're available from their makers.

  • The Emotional Culture Deck (opens in new tab) · by riders&elephants
    How are we feeling as a team, and how do we want to feel?
  • More than Words (opens in new tab) · by Sabrina David
    What does this picture say that we don't have words for, yet?
  • Deeper Questions (opens in new tab) · by Kiran Patel

The worksheets adapt these tools rather than reproduce them, which is why each is credited "after" its maker. None of the tools are mine, and it's not a complete set. Bring your own into the story.
